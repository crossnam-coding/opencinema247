Now on the marquee.

Five wide-release Korean features, 2021–2024, as they played in theaters — full-length, with subtitles. Availability may vary by country.

NEW YEAR BLUES

새해전야 New Year Blues

2021Romance · ComedyDir. Hong Ji-young

Four couples, one romantic and wobbly week leading up to New Year's Eve.

Cast: Kim Kang-woo, Yoo In-na, Yoo Yeon-seok, Lee Yeon-hee, Lee Dong-hwi, Choi Soo-young, Yoo Teo

REMEMBER

리멤버 Remember

2022Action · ThrillerDir. Lee Il-hyung

An 80-year-old with Alzheimer's sets out to finish the revenge he has planned for sixty years — with a clueless twenty-something behind the wheel.

Cast: Lee Sung-min, Nam Joo-hyuk

TROLL FACTORY

댓글부대 Troll Factory

2024Crime · DramaDir. Ahn Gook-jin

A suspended reporter uncovers the troll factory manipulating public opinion, one fake comment at a time.

Cast: Son Suk-ku, Kim Sung-cheol, Kim Dong-hwi, Hong Kyung

DIRTY MONEY

더러운 돈에 손대지 마라 Dirty Money

2024Action · CrimeDir. Kim Min-su

Two detectives steal a criminal fortune — and land inside the very investigation they are supposed to be running.

Cast: Jung Woo, Kim Dae-myung, Park Byung-eun

FIREFIGHTERS

소방관 Firefighters

2024Disaster · DramaDir. Kwak Kyung-taek

Based on the true story of the 2001 Hongje-dong fire — the crew that went in when everyone else ran out.

Cast: Joo Won, Kwak Do-won, Yoo Jae-myung

Why watch here

Not clips. Not rips.
The whole film.

  1. 01

    Officially licensed

    Every film is here under an agreement with its rights holder and registered with YouTube Content ID. The people who made it get paid.

  2. 02

    The theatrical cut

    Start to finish, as it played in cinemas. No ten-minute recaps, no cam rips, no “part 3 of 9”.

  3. 03

    Subtitles that make sense

    Proper English subtitles first — more languages as the library grows.

  4. 04

    Free. Always open.

    Ad-supported, no subscription, no sign-up. Three in the morning is a perfectly good time for a movie.

Eokmanjangja Pictures
